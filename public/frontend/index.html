Our Story

Swiss Engineering,
Built for India

Founded in 1974 by Alfred E. Schiller, SCHILLER grew from a one-room operation into a global group with 31 subsidiaries. In 1997, Schiller Healthcare India was established with a singular mission: bringing world-class medical technology within reach of every Indian healthcare provider.

Today, our ISO 13485-certified production centre in Puducherry manufactures a range of devices complementing the Swiss parent portfolio. Our R&D hub in Bengaluru develops 2–3 new products annually, keeping the range current with clinical needs.
